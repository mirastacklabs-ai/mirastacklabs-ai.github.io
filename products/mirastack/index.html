One Platform for Technology and Business Signals

Traditional monitoring vendors optimize for a single dimension — metrics, logs, or traces — and treat business data as somebody else's problem. Business-intelligence vendors do the inverse, remaining blind to the systems that generate the revenue. MIRASTACK collapses that divide into a single, self-hosted platform where operational telemetry and business data live side by side.

Unifying the Operational and Commercial Divide

MIRASTACK is engineered as the consolidation point. It connects to purpose-built observability stores and to SQL-warehouse-class business backends through the same integration model, renders both through a single visual experience, and lets an AI assistant investigate across every signal without the analyst ever writing a query.

  • Open Data Foundation: Standardized on VictoriaMetrics, VictoriaLogs, and VictoriaTraces for telemetry, with ClickHouse, OpenSearch, and S3 for analytical workloads.
  • Autonomous Intelligence (MIRA): Investigation, root-cause analysis, correlation, prediction, and SLO reasoning accessible in natural language.
  • Generative UI: Bring your own model on-premises (Ollama, vLLM) or use commercial APIs; results render as interactive React components.

The Architecture of Sovereignty

Four tightly integrated pillars define the MIRASTACK Platform, enabling high-performance telemetry collection, no-code visualization, agentic AI reasoning, and a hardened alerting plane.

Pillar 1: Unified Data Platform & BI Consolidation

A data source is a first-class, pluggable concept rather than a hard-wired assumption. Connect many data sources centrally and combine technical and business signals on a single canvas, positioning MIRASTACK as a direct consolidation path for standalone BI tools.

  • Per-widget source binding: metrics, logs, traces, and SQL co-exist
  • Sovereign by construction: no data round-trip to a vendor cloud
  • Cost-efficient storage: VictoriaMetrics compresses metrics 10–20× better than alternatives
DIAGRAM: Open, Interoperable, Cost-Effective Storage Pillar 1

Pillar 2: Data Studio No-Code Composer

The operator-facing composer that sits upstream of everything visual in MIRASTACK. Deliberately designed so that the learning curve is flat: an analyst who understands the question can build the visual without first learning a query language.

  • No-code visual builders for metrics, logs, traces, and ClickHouse SQL
  • Ten visualization types: time series, bar, single-value, tables, log views, and more
  • Live preview, autosaved drafts, and guided onboarding tours
SCREENSHOT: Data Studio 3-Pane Workspace Pillar 2

Pillar 3: MIRA Agentic Intelligence

Every observability query is accessible through natural language via MIRA. Ask in English; MIRA routes your query through appropriate agents, correlates signals, and synthesizes answers with evidence.

  • 7+ specialized agents: failure detection, correlation, RCA, service graph, and more
  • Three execution lanes (Conversational, Workflow DAG, and Augmented)
  • Generative UI: 18 component kinds streamed in real-time (charts, tables, DAGs, traces)
SCREENSHOT: Three Execution Lanes UI Pillar 3

Pillar 4: Alerting & Operate Control Plane

A complete alerting and on-call control plane built on a hardened fork of Prometheus Alertmanager v0.32.x. Alerting configurations are governed, versioned, and testable as application code.

  • Multi-tenancy: each tenant owns its own alertmanager configuration
  • Sealed secrets: AES-256-GCM encrypted receiver credentials
  • Operate Portal: live stream, escalation policies, rotations, and SLO management

Open Standards & Collaborations

MIRASTACK is built on open standards to prevent vendor lock-in and ensure data portability. We actively collaborate with open-source ecosystems like VictoriaMetrics.

Interoperable and Future-Proof

Our telemetry ingestion is 100% OpenTelemetry-native, and our metrics engine is fully Prometheus PromQL compatible. We contribute back to the open-source community, licensing our core observability agents under AGPL v3.

  • OpenTelemetry Ingest: Native support for OTLP metrics, logs, and traces.
  • Prometheus & Jaeger APIs: 100% compatible with existing dashboards and Jaeger clients.
  • AGPL v3 Agents: Open-source agents for correlation, RCA, anomaly detection, and service graphs.
