self.hosted.providers

Self-hosted providers

Run open-weight models on your GPUs inside your network. The platform supports vLLM, llama.cpp, Ollama, and any OpenAI-compatible inference endpoint. Model weights never leave your perimeter.

Each self-hosted provider runs as a tenant-isolated process. It is discovered by the router at startup and registered with its capabilities, context window, and cost basis. The provider process remains alive for the entire tenant lifetime — no per-request construction, no shared state across tenants.

frontier.providers

Frontier providers

Frontier model APIs are reachable through the same router, the same RBAC, and the same audit trail as self-hosted models. The difference is the policy — not the path.

Frontier providers are configured by the operator, not by the end user. The routing decision applies data-classification rules before the call leaves the perimeter. Regulated payloads never leave the perimeter, by rule rather than by convention.

resolution.order

Resolution order

The router resolves every request through a deterministic order: policy first, then availability, then cost. The operator defines the policy. The router enforces it.

1. Request enters router
↓
2. Data classification evaluated
↓
3. Policy decides target provider
↓
4. Provider availability checked
↓
5. Dispatch executed
↓
6. Token and cost recorded
data.classification.routing

Data-classification routing

Every payload is classified before it leaves the router. The classification is set by the operator and cannot be overridden by the agent or the user.

PUBLIC

Public

May route to self-hosted or frontier models. No additional restrictions.

INTERNAL

Internal

Routes to self-hosted models by default. Frontier escalation requires explicit policy approval and logged justification.

CONFIDENTIAL

Confidential

Must route to self-hosted models only. Frontier APIs are blocked at the router level. Approval required for any model access.

RESTRICTED

Restricted

Highest classification. Requires dual approval and is restricted to designated model endpoints only. Full audit trail generated.

token.and.cost.ledger

The token and cost ledger

Prompt and completion tokens, cost, and latency are recorded per turn — whether the response streamed or buffered. Both paths report through a single accounting chokepoint, so they can never diverge.

The ledger is attached to the same immutable audit trail as the governance event. An auditor can reconstruct the full cost of any agentic workflow — per model, per tenant, per action — without exporting logs or joining tables.
