What an AI Factory actually is

A GPU cluster is not an AI Factory. It is the floor the factory stands on.

Models

Open-weight models on your GPUs. Frontier models by policy. One router, one ledger.

Tools

MCP servers as governed runtimes, not laptop processes. Classified, approved, scoped.

Agents

Personas with explicit tool mappings. Only approved capabilities reach the planner.

Governance

EGAProtocol. One dispatch gate for every plugin call and every tool call.

Evidence

Every token, cost, decision, approval, and actor — attributable and replayable.

The factory is what turns raw capability into governed, accountable work.

deployment.topology

Deployment topology

MIRASTACK runs where your data must stay. The platform is designed from the ground up for sovereign, air-gapped, and restricted-egress environments.

Air-gapped — no internet route

Fully operational with zero internet connectivity. All model weights, tool runtimes, and telemetry backends are deployed inside the perimeter. No outbound calls are required for the platform to function.

On-premise — controlled egress

Self-hosted model inference through vLLM, llama.cpp, or compatible runtimes. Frontier API calls are routed through the same governance gate as internal models, with data-classification controls that prevent regulated payloads from leaving the perimeter.

Hybrid — on-prem plus frontier APIs

Sensitive workloads stay inside the perimeter. Explicitly approved tasks may call frontier models through policy-defined escalation. The routing decision is governed, not configured by the end user.

Private cloud / VPC

Deployed in your private cloud or VPC with full tenant isolation. The platform does not depend on shared tenancy or external SaaS control planes. Every component runs under your identity and access management.
